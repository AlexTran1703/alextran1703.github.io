Khanh Duy Tran

I am an enthusiastic researcher interested in the technical feasibility of wearable biomedical platforms. The question that keeps pulling me back is simple: does an engineered system sense reliably, and can what it measures become directly meaningful for prospective clinical use?

I am not especially drawn to technology for its own sake. Outside research, I am more naturally an art enthusiast, especially when it comes to music, along with books and stories that make me think.

“The day I die is the day I am no longer able to hear the music.”
